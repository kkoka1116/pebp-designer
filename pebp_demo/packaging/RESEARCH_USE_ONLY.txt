PEBP Studio - RESEARCH USE ONLY. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.

PEBP Studio (PEBP Designer) is research and teaching software for designing the pre-epiglottic baton
plate (Tuebingen palatal plate). It is not a medical device. It has not been cleared or approved by any
regulator, and it has not been clinically validated. Nothing designed with it has yet been printed,
cast or bench-tested.

Do not use it to diagnose or treat any patient, and do not fit a device made from its output to a
patient on the strength of this software. Every clinical choice (cleft type, tip target, sleep-study
index and threshold, route, sign-off) belongs to the clinician. The software stores and flags those
choices; it never makes them.

Patient data: real intraoral scans and everything made from them are patient data. PEBP Studio works
only on this computer (it answers requests from 127.0.0.1 only) and sends nothing anywhere. Keep its
folder out of iCloud Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox and email.
